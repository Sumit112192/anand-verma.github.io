About Me

Yogi | Engineer | IIT Delhi Graduate

My journey began in the vibrant learning spaces of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas-first in Kaimur and later in Pune. These schools shaped my spirit of curiosity, discipline, and teamwork. I was fortunate to top the 10th class across all JNVs in India, and to represent my school in National Integration Meets and Yoga sports competitions. Those years not only gave me academic confidence but also instilled in me the value of community and harmony.

This path took me to IIT Delhi, where I pursued Electrical Engineering. Beyond the classroom, I represented the Fine Arts Club, participated in a Road Safety Campaign, and explored the joy of creativity through art. My curiosity for science also deepened, leading to research in hardware implementation of neural networks, where I worked on designing energy-efficient on-chip learning systems. Some of this work was published and presented on international platforms-my Google Scholar profile has the details.

After graduation, I began my professional career with Qualcomm as an engineering intern and later joined Microsoft, where I spent three enriching years in the Cloud + AI Commerce division. Working on large-scale enterprise systems taught me how technology can touch millions of lives when paired with vision and responsibility. Each role pushed me to grow not just as an engineer, but as a problem-solver who thrives at the intersection of technology and impact.

Over time, I felt equally drawn to the inner journey. I pursued a Post Graduate Diploma in Yogic Studies at Nalanda, where I explored philosophy, meditation, and the art of balance. Beyond academics and work, I find joy in painting-a practice that helps me see the world with fresh colors and perspectives. For me, technology, yoga, and art are not separate threads but part of a single tapestry- one that blends innovation with introspection.
